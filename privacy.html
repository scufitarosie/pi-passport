Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2025

Pi Passport (“the App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our App inside the Pi Network ecosystem.

1. Information We Collect

  • Basic Pi Account Data: Only your username and user ID (uid) are accessed through the official Pi SDK.
  • Optional Passport Data: Any endorsements, activity inputs, or notes you add are stored locally in your browser. They are never shared with us.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your Pi username and UID are used only to verify your identity with Pi Network’s official API. No other personal information is collected or stored.

3. Data Storage

- We do not maintain servers that store personal data permanently.
- All sensitive operations are performed via Pi’s secure API.
- Passport details are stored only on your device (local storage).

4. Sharing of Data

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. Data is only transmitted to Pi Network’s official API for verification purposes.

5. Your Control

You may delete your local Passport data at any time by clearing your browser storage or reinstalling Pi Browser.
